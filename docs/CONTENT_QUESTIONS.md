# Content Questions

These questions remain open; Section 1 does not silently decide them.

1. **Campaign geography** — Is the public campaign India-wide, Mumbai-only, or a city-by-city Mirchi initiative? The deck says “India,” “across the city,” “Mirchi Mumbai,” and “our cities” in different places.
2. **Meaning of 983** — Does `983` represent unique trees, approved promises, participating people, a one-time Mirchi seed activity, or a combination? How are duplicates handled?
3. **Guardian wording** — Is the approved certificate label “Guardian of Tree No.” or “Vriksha Guardian No.”?
4. **Submissions after 983** — The deck says the movement can continue beyond 983. Should the public tracker accept and display promises after the target, and how should progress be represented?
5. **Meet-and-greet rules** — What are the eligibility, geography, age, entry period, judging/draw method, prize details, consent, publicity and legal terms? No meet-and-greet CTA is published in Section 1.
6. **Language requirements** — Is the final experience English-only, bilingual English/Hindi, or multilingual? Which script should be used for Hindi copy?
7. **Campaign dates** — Are 21–28 August approved, what year applies, and which dates are public-facing versus internal media planning dates?
8. **Celebrity imagery and video** — Are Sidharth Malhotra and Tamannaah Bhatia visuals, names, likenesses, footage and social content cleared for public website use? Which supplied files/URLs are approved?
9. **Logo package** — Can Mirchi provide an approved standalone Vriksha Vvandhan wordmark, icon/favicon and lockup rules?
10. **Audio approvals** — Which Ped Ka Paigaam recordings, transcripts, RJ names and usage rights are approved for the website?
11. **Certificate/legal meaning** — What legal disclaimer should accompany “Guardian” or “adopted tree” language so it cannot imply ownership, land rights, government endorsement or maintenance authority?
12. **Image provenance** — Are all deck-generated or composited campaign visuals cleared for external public use, or should final photography replace them before launch?
