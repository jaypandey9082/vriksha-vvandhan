# Content and Policy Decisions

## Resolved for implementation

- Public fields are exactly display name, email, one photograph, publication consent and terms acceptance.
- Public participants have no accounts.
- Reviewer and Admin may review and approve in the future portal.
- Reviewer may only recommend rejection and must write a participant-facing comment.
- Reviewer cannot finalise rejection; the submission becomes **Rejection Awaiting Admin**.
- Admin may confirm rejection, approve instead, or reject directly with a participant-facing comment.
- There is no Return to Reviewer option.
- A rejection email is eligible only after Admin confirmation or direct Admin rejection.
- The approved certificate label is **Vriksha Guardian No.**
- Admin controls Trash, restore and permanent deletion.
- The future count is derived only from active Published, non-test records that count toward the goal.
